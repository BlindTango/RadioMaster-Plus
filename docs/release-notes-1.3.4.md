# RadioMaster+ 1.3.4

A context menu for YouTube results, and a podcast refresh focus fix.

## YouTube
- The results list has a context menu (right-click, Shift+F10 or the Applications key): Play, Download Video, Download Audio Only, Download All Videos from Channel, Load More Videos and Subscribe to Channel.
- Download All Videos from Channel lists every upload of the channel you are browsing (or the selected video's channel), reports how many it found, and asks whether to queue them as video, as audio only, or not at all. Large channels can take a while to list.

## Podcasts
- After Refresh Episodes from the episode context menu, focus and selection return to the episode list instead of being lost when the list reloads.

## Known limits
- The new menu items and the quality label from 1.3.3 are not yet translated.
- One test in the suite (a 50,000-row station-health list) fails intermittently in full runs; it passes on its own.
